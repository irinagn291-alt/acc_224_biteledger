BiteLedger is a personal food ledger that posts every bite as a debit against the day's energy budget. It is for anyone who wants calorie and macro tracking framed as a ruled book on the device.

• Ledger shows today's energy, running balance, and Protein, Carbs, and Fat against your targets, with Opening Entry, Midday Entry, Closing Entry, and Petty Cash slots
• Lookup searches the public Open Food Facts catalogue (plus a bundled local shelf when the wire is down)
• Scan reads packet barcodes with the camera, or you can type a barcode and Post code
• Folio lets you set portion grams, then Assign to Post entry as Eaten today or Plan ahead
• Statement lists the day's lines with a balance column and Export CSV
• Planner covers the next 14 days; convert a plan with Post as eaten today
• Wishlist saves a folio when you mean to buy it
• Goals sets Energy kcal, Protein g, Carbs g, and Fat g, with Reset all data and Contact BiteLedger

Ledger data is stored on the device. No account is required.
