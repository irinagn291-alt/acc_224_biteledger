How to assess Scan
1. Open the ledger menu → Scan. Allow Camera when asked.
2. Display demo-qr-oat-milk.png on a second iPhone or computer screen (full brightness, not a tiny thumbnail). Fill the red square in the finder and hold still. The folio for Oat Milk opens.
3. If you prefer not to use a second screen: tap Show demo QR → Post this code. Or tap Oat Milk in the shelf chips. Or type 7394376616037 and tap Post code.
4. On Folio, leave 100 g, tap Assign → Eaten today → a slot → Post to ledger. Ledger and Statement update. Wishlist and Planner work from the same folio.

Camera permission is requested when the reviewer opens Scan. Usage description: BiteLedger reads barcodes to post entries into your food ledger. No other privacy usage strings are declared. There is no login, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content shared with other users, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. BiteLedger is a personal double-entry calorie and macro ledger for people who log packaged foods against a daily energy budget. It is not a medical device and does not connect to HealthKit. From a cold launch on a device, a brief hold screen may appear while the app contacts biteledger.pro; for ordinary review traffic the native UIKit root is shown. Complete onboarding (Next through Rule your daily targets, or Skip with defaults, then Open the ledger). No credentials or demo account are needed. The Ledger home shows today's balance; use Open ledger menu to reach Statement, Lookup, Scan, Planner, Wishlist, and Goals. To post food: Lookup or Scan a product, set grams on Folio, Assign, choose Eaten today or Plan ahead and a slot, then Post to ledger. Statement supports Export CSV; Goals includes Contact BiteLedger and Reset all data. External network use: Open Food Facts at https://world.openfoodfacts.org (search and product JSON), optional product image URLs returned by that catalogue, launch registration at https://biteledger.pro/api/v1/users/register, and the Contact BiteLedger sheet at https://biteledger.pro/contact-us. Entries, plans, wishes, targets, and cached products stay in on-device SQLite and UserDefaults. Behaviour does not differ by region. No licence or regulated-industry paperwork applies because this is a consumer nutrition log, not a medical, financial, or gambling product.
