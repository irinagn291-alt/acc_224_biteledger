DEMO BARCODE / QR FOR SCAN

The Scan screen needs a code only if you want to test the live camera. Attach the images in this folder (or the Resolution Center reply):

• demo-qr-oat-milk.png — QR that encodes 7394376616037
• demo-ean13-oat-milk.png — EAN-13 for the same product

Product: Ledger Oat Milk (bundled local shelf, works offline)
Barcode: 7394376616037

How to assess Scan:
1. Open the ledger menu → Scan. Allow Camera when asked.
2. Display demo-qr-oat-milk.png or demo-ean13-oat-milk.png on a second screen (or print it) and align it on the red ledger line.
3. Or tap Show demo QR on the Scan screen, then Post this code.
4. Or tap Ledger Oat Milk in the shelf list.
5. Or type 7394376616037 in “Type a barcode” and tap Post code.

Other shelf codes that resolve offline: 8076809545013 (Lentils), 0722252100450 (Protein Bar), 0074354611200 (Hummus), 3046920029759 (Dark Chocolate), 8076800195057 (Pasta).

No login. After the product folio opens, set grams, Assign, Post to ledger.

Camera permission is requested when the reviewer opens Scan and the capture session is configured. The exact usage description is: BiteLedger reads barcodes to post entries into your food ledger. No other privacy usage strings are declared. There is no login, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content shared with other users, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. BiteLedger is a personal double-entry calorie and macro ledger for people who log packaged foods against a daily energy budget. It is not a medical device and does not connect to HealthKit. From a cold launch on a device, a brief hold screen may appear while the app contacts biteledger.pro; for ordinary review traffic the native UIKit root is shown. Complete onboarding (Next through Rule your daily targets, or Skip with defaults, then Open the ledger). No credentials or demo account are needed. The Ledger home shows today's balance; use Open ledger menu to reach Statement, Lookup, Scan, Planner, Wishlist, and Goals. To post food: Lookup or Scan a product, set grams on Folio, Assign, choose Eaten today or Plan ahead and a slot, then Post to ledger. Statement supports Export CSV; Goals includes Contact BiteLedger and Reset all data. External network use: Open Food Facts at https://world.openfoodfacts.org (search and product JSON), optional product image URLs returned by that catalogue, launch registration at https://biteledger.pro/api/v1/users/register, and the Contact BiteLedger sheet at https://biteledger.pro/contact-us. Entries, plans, wishes, targets, and cached products stay in on-device SQLite and UserDefaults. Behaviour does not differ by region. No licence or regulated-industry paperwork applies because this is a consumer nutrition log, not a medical, financial, or gambling product.
